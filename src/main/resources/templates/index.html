Built for trust at every step

From registration to the final count, each stage is protected and open to verification.

Biometric authentication

A live face capture is matched against the voter's registered template before the ballot opens.

Encrypted ballots

Each vote is encrypted with AES-256 and tagged with a keyed hash, so the choice stays secret.

Tamper-proof ledger

Votes are mined into blocks linked by SHA-256 hashes. Changing one block breaks the chain.

One voter, one vote

An anonymous voter token on the chain blocks any second vote in the same election.

Public audit

Every voter gets a transaction ID and can confirm their vote on the public ledger.

Verified results

The chain is validated before counting, and results are published only if it is intact.

How it works

Four steps from registration to an auditable result.

  1. 1

    Register

    Enter your details and capture your face. The election authority approves your registration.

  2. 2

    Verify identity

    Before voting, your live face is compared with your stored biometric template.

  3. 3

    Cast your vote

    Your choice is encrypted, hashed and mined into a new block on the chain.

  4. 4

    Audit the result

    Check your receipt on the public ledger and view the verified result.
